RK BRO CREATIVE — WEBSITE STARTER

CONTENTS
- index.html: website structure and page content
- style.css: responsive dark / electric-blue design
- script.js: mobile menu, gallery filters and enquiry form
- assets/: place your own original artwork here

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to preview it in Chrome or Edge.

HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN WALLPAPERS
1. Export your ORIGINAL artwork from Adobe Illustrator as JPG or PNG.
2. Copy the image into the assets folder.
3. Open index.html in a text editor.
4. Replace each sample .wall-art preview block with an image, for example:
   <div class="wall-art"><img src="assets/my-wallpaper.jpg" alt="My original wallpaper"></div>
5. Add this CSS rule to style.css:
   .wall-art img { width:100%; height:100%; object-fit:cover; display:block; }

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Replace sample preview art with designs you own or are licensed to sell.
- In script.js, replace ADD_PARENT_MANAGED_EMAIL_HERE with a contact email managed by your parent/guardian.
- Have a parent/guardian review the website, enquiries, privacy and payment arrangements.
- This starter site does NOT process payments, store customer data, or automatically deliver downloads.
- Do not publish your home address, school, personal phone number, passwords or private information.
- Do not sell copyrighted game/movie logos, characters, screenshots or wallpapers unless you have permission.
- Check the website host's age and account rules with your parent/guardian.

PUBLISHING
You can publish this static website using a hosting provider, but account ownership, domain purchases and payment setup should be handled by a parent/guardian. The site is plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript and does not require a database.
